## What the numbers say (n = 14)
- **Saturated colour inside the app window:** 0.2–2.1% of pixels on 13 of 14 screens. The colour is in the frame or the content, not in the chrome.
- **Filled (solid) buttons:** at most **1** on every screen, and **0** on 7. The brand colour is spent on that one primary.
- **Type:** one sans on all 14, plus at most a mono for IDs or one serif title. Page titles are 20–30px, often light or regular weight. Hierarchy comes from grey ink levels on 11 of 14.
- **Grouping:** 1px borders on 8 of 14. Shadows appear only on floating layers (menus, popovers, panels).
- **Selection:** a neutral grey fill on 8 of 14. No screen colours its sidebar.
- **Status:** at most 3 semantic colours, as a dot, an icon or text, rather than filled pills.
- **AI:** shown as a named participant with provenance ("Thinking…", "2 sources", an agent tag). None of the 14 uses sparkle or gradient decoration.
- **Never observed:** textured or gradient sidebars, a brand-coloured sidebar, coloured page headers, a mascot on the working surface, brand gradients on buttons, a KPI-card row (12 of 14 have none).

## Where the world goes when you show the app
In marketing screenshots, the product page, onboarding or login, the world **frames the window from outside**: a painting, collage, gradient or photo behind or beside the app (Cursor, Granola, Linear docs, Ramp). Inside the window, the app stays neutral. This is the right way to combine a strong Persuade world with a clean Operate UI.
