# Defining Business

## Overview

Buyers pay for outcomes, not services. A business is defined when one niche, its pains in the buyer's own words, the mirrored outcomes, a named mechanism, a guarantee, outcome-tied deliverables and cost-based pricing all fit on one sheet.

**Deliverable = the Business Definition Sheet below, sections 1–9 in order.** Write it in the user's language (translate the slot labels too).

- If the user asked only to define the business → end after section 9 with one line offering the next step (outbound, copy, website, contracts).
- If the user also asked for something else (cold email, landing page…) → deliver the sheet first, then do that request built from the sheet, using its matching skill if one exists.

## When to Use

- Turning a skill or freelance work into a sellable service
- Offer reads as "we do cold email / video editing / bookkeeping"
- Pricing is a guess, or there is no guarantee
- Not for: writing the emails, ads or landing page (do this first, then hand off)

## The Order (each step feeds the next)

Service → Niche → Pain → Outcome → USP → Guarantee → Deliverables → Pricing → One-liner.
Pain before offer: once the pain is clear, the offer writes itself.

## Business Definition Sheet (the output contract)

```markdown
# [Business name]

## 1. Service
Bán gì: [service]
Bằng chứng mình làm được: [result / case study it already has]

## 2. Niche
Ai: [role] tại [loại công ty, quy mô]
Vì sao chọn: [có tiền, pain rõ, dễ build list]

## 3. Top 5 Pain (bằng từ của khách)
1. "[câu khách tự nói]" → Implication: [mất bao nhiêu tiền / giờ / doanh thu]
... (đúng 5)
Nguồn: [lời khách thật | GIẢ THUYẾT – cần verify qua sales call, review đối thủ, forum ngành]

## 4. Desired Outcomes (mirror 1-1 với Pain)
1. [trạng thái sau, có số hoặc mốc thời gian] ← Pain 1
... (đúng 5, cùng thứ tự)

## 5. USP
[Tên mechanism 2–3 chữ]™: [nó hoạt động thế nào, 1 câu]

## 6. Guarantee
[Tên guarantee]: Nếu không [kết quả đo được] trong [thời gian] thì [refund / làm tiếp miễn phí / credit]
Điều kiện: [khách phải làm gì để guarantee có hiệu lực]

## 7. Deliverables (đủ để đạt Outcome, không hơn)
- [Hạng mục] → phục vụ Outcome [#] – [giờ] × [rate] (+ chi phí tool) = $[cost]
...

## 8. Pricing
COGS: $[tổng mục 7]
Retail (COGS × 3): $[giá]
Gói bán: $[giá] pay in full cho [kỳ]; [tùy chọn trả góp cao hơn retail]

## 9. One-liner
We help [niche] get [outcome] in [timeframe] without [pain] using [USP] — or [guarantee].
```

Missing facts (rates, results, market) → put a reasonable number and tag it `[giả định]`; list open questions in one short line after the sheet.

## Quick Reference: sharp vs blurry

| Slot | Blurry | Sharp |
|---|---|---|
| Niche | SMEs, B2B companies | Marketing agencies 5–30 staff, founder still selling |
| Pain | "Lack of leads" | "Feast or famine" → 2 tháng không có deal = trễ lương |
| Outcome | More clients | Pipeline không phụ thuộc referral, 60 warm lead / 3 tháng |
| USP | Fast, quality, dedicated | Outbound Authority Blueprint |
| Pricing | Tiers $450/$850/$1,500 | COGS $1,450 × 3 → $4,500; $6,000 pay in full / 3 tháng |

**Test each Pain/Outcome line:** would the buyer say it to a peer? Is it measurable? Swap in another niche — still true? Then it's too generic. Mentions a tool or task? Rewrite as a result.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Unrequested outbound plan, scripts, KPIs | Stop at section 9; offer the next step in one line |
| Refusing an extra the user explicitly asked for | Sheet first, then that extra, built from the sheet |
| Outcomes don't match pains one-for-one | Number them; Outcome N answers Pain N |
| Pain stated as fact when invented | Mark source GIẢ THUYẾT |
| USP = list of adjectives | One named mechanism, 2–3 words |
| Price picked from "market rates" | Build from deliverable hours × rate, then × 3 |
| Deliverables list everything you could do | Each item names the outcome it serves; drop the rest |
| Several niches "to keep options open" | One niche per sheet; another niche = another sheet |
